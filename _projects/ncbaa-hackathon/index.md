## Challenge
At the NCBAA Hackathon, our team explored how aviation research and AI could support safer, more sustainable operations and stronger community outcomes.

## My contribution
I researched and organized aviation material for the platform, helped shape the project concept, and refined how we presented the solution. As a team, we built an interactive website to make the information accessible.

## Result
Our project earned first place in the NCBAA track. We presented it to judges and representatives from Google, Adobe, IBM, and other organizations.
