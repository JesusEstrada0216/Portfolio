## Project
Our team needed a sheet metal housing to package and protect its high voltage electronics. When the original designer shifted to other priorities, I took initiative to carry the enclosure through design and fabrication.

## Design decisions
I modeled the enclosure in SolidWorks Sheet Metal using 0.090-inch Aluminum 5052. I selected 5052 for its low weight and suitability for bending and forming. Compared with 6061, it was a better fit for the bends required and less prone to cracking during fabrication. Because the housing was an electronics enclosure rather than a load-bearing structure, I focused the design on fit, fabrication, and protection.

## Outcome
The completed housing met the project requirements and securely protected the electronics package.
