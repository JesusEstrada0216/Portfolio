## Project
As a Student Intern Team Leader, I helped guide a multidisciplinary team developing a Complete Streets proposal for Los Angeles's Vermont Avenue corridor.

## My contribution
I coordinated team responsibilities and helped guide research and design discussions. Using 3DStreet and SolidWorks, I helped visualize proposed roadway, pedestrian, transit, and landscaping improvements.

## Proposal
The concept focused on safer bike lanes, wider sidewalks, dedicated bus lanes, improved transit stops, native plants, and better access near the Vermont/Athens C-Line station.
