About Me

My interest in engineering comes from enjoying the process of understanding how things work and finding ways to improve them. As a first-generation college student, I have learned to create opportunities for myself by staying curious, asking questions, and continuously building new skills.

San José State University has given me the opportunity to grow both technically and personally. I value environments where I can learn from experienced people, contribute to a team, and take responsibility for producing quality work.

Outside of engineering I enjoy building gaming computers and playing soccer.
What drives me Curiosity, persistence, and turning ideas into something real.
